I am Junteng Liu, a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.hkust.edu.hk/), led by Professor [Junxian He](https://www.linkedin.com/in/junxianhe/). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in LLM reasoning, reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models, and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

I also serve as a Research Intern at [MINIMAX](https://minimax.tech/) (February 2025 - Present).
