I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I work in the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He (who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU). My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML).

Education
======
* **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024–Present
* **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), 2020–2024 (graduated June 2024)
* **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University
