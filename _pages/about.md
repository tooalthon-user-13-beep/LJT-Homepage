# Junteng Liu

Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://home.cse.ust.hk/~junxianh/). I received my B.Eng. from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was previously advised by Prof. Junxian He as an undergraduate.

## Research

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Developing methods to enhance the logical reasoning capabilities of large language models through reasoning data synthesis and reinforcement learning.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Investigating the perception bottlenecks of VLMs and developing methods to reduce hallucinations in multimodal models.
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Understanding the internal representations of LLMs to identify truthfulness-related structures and improve model reliability.
