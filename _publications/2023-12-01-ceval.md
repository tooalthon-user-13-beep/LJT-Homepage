C-Eval is a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. It covers 52 diverse disciplines across four difficulty levels (Middle School, High School, College, and Professional). C-Eval provides a rigorous benchmark for evaluating the capabilities of large language models on Chinese-language tasks.
