This paper discovers a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside LLMs. We find that the representation space of LLMs contains a linear subspace that governs the truthfulness of generated responses. By manipulating representations along this hyperplane, we can modulate the truthfulness of LLMs. This work provides a fundamental understanding of how LLMs encode truthfulness information.
