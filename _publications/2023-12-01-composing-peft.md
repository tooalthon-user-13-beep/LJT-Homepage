This paper introduces methods for composing parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) modules with arithmetic operations. We demonstrate that PEFT modules learned on different tasks can be combined via arithmetic operations to enable efficient multi-task adaptation, significantly reducing the computational cost of adapting to new tasks.

Paper: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00000)
