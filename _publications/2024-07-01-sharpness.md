This paper proposes in-context sharpness as a signal for detecting and mitigating hallucinations in LLMs. We find that the sharpness of the model's representations when processing context tokens can serve as an alert signal for potential hallucinations, enabling more reliable hallucination mitigation strategies.

Paper: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00000)
